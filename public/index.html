How it works

  1. Pick your field on the map and tell us the crop, planting date and soil.
  2. The app downloads the last 14 days and the next 7 days of weather for that spot (Open-Meteo), including reference evapotranspiration.
  3. It tracks how much water is left in the root zone each day (FAO-56 water balance) and tells you when the crop will need water and how much.
  4. Optionally, AI explains the plan in plain English, Amharic or Afaan Oromo.

Example plan: a wheat field on farmland near Bishoftu, planted 55 days ago, clay soil. Tap the map to plan your own field.
